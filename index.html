Warranty & Returns Policy

3-year worldwide warranty

Every product purchased from TXT WORLDWIDE SHOP is covered by our own 3-year warranty, valid worldwide. If a device develops a manufacturing defect, we repair or replace it free of charge — in any country. The warranty period starts on the delivery date; please keep your order number as proof of purchase.

Worldwide coverage & support

Our warranty travels with the product across borders. Wherever you are, contact us with your order number and we'll arrange service through our international support network.

14-day returns

Changed your mind? Return any unused item in its original packaging within 14 days of delivery for a full refund. Return shipping instructions are provided with your order.

What the warranty excludes

Accidental or liquid damage, unauthorised repairs, and normal wear are not covered. Consumable parts such as batteries follow standard manufacturer terms. Statutory consumer rights are unaffected.
